Speech & NLP ·

What if your speech model has never heard tone?

Hundreds of millions of people speak tonal African languages. Every major speech model ignores the one signal that makes them work: the pitch. JALI is our attempt to fix that, from first principles.

  • Tonal African languages: tone is lexical, not prosodic. A structural problem, not a data problem.
  • Existing SSL models (HuBERT, MMS, wav2vec 2.0) discard pitch implicitly through quantization
  • JALI proposes a dual-branch architecture that treats F0 as a first-class signal
  • Full prior architectural specification published, see link below

AI & Health · Hackathon National IA & Santé Sans Réseau — 1st place

A child is breathing too fast. Can you count precisely enough to know?

In rural Morocco, diagnosing pneumonia in a child comes down to counting breaths by eye for 60 seconds. The literature shows this is harder than it sounds. NAFS won a national hackathon for automating it, offline, on a regular smartphone. A pilot is now underway.

  • Respiratory rate measured automatically from 30 seconds of chest video
  • Cough audio classified by a lightweight CNN trained on COUGHVID and Coswara
  • Fully offline, runs on smartphone or shared Raspberry Pi via local WiFi
  • Aligned to WHO IMCI thresholds, designed as a decision support tool, not a diagnosis
  • First place at the National Hackathon for AI in Disconnected Healthcare Settings

Bayesian AI · Published

Rule-State Inference: A Bayesian Framework for Compliance Monitoring

A novel Bayesian framework that inverts the standard ML paradigm: treating authoritative rules as structured priors to infer latent compliance states from partial, noisy observations.

  • Novel formulation: rules as priors, compliance as latent state
  • O(1) regulatory change absorption (no retraining)
  • AUC 0.859 on Togolese fiscal compliance benchmark
  • Domain-agnostic: tax, health protocols, AML, environmental regulation
